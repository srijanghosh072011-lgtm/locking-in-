Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your personal information — in plain English.

Last updated: July 2026. This policy is a starting template — have it reviewed by a lawyer before relying on it.

1. Who we are

Plumbo Plumbing & Heating ("we", "us") operates this website and provides plumbing services in Regina, Saskatchewan, Canada. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this site.

2. What we collect

  • Contact details you submit through our forms: name, phone number, email address, and the service you're asking about.
  • Newsletter email if you subscribe (with your express consent, per CASL).
  • Technical data such as anonymized analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies via our consent banner.

3. How we use it

We use your information solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule and perform work, invoice you, and — only with consent — send occasional service reminders or newsletters. We do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

4. Legal basis & compliance

We handle personal information in accordance with Canada's PIPEDA and Saskatchewan privacy legislation, and our commercial electronic messages comply with CASL: every marketing email includes our identity, contact details and a working unsubscribe link, honoured within 10 days.

5. Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics cookies are set only after you click "Accept" on the consent banner; declining changes nothing about how the site works for you.

6. Retention & security

Enquiry data is kept only as long as needed to serve you and meet tax/warranty record obligations, then deleted. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored with reputable providers using access controls.

7. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing hello@plumboregina.ca. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time using the unsubscribe link or by contacting us.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: Plumbo Plumbing & Heating, 2310 Albert Street, Regina, SK S4P 2V7 · hello@plumboregina.ca · (306) 555-0142.
