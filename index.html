What types of businesses do you work with?

From first-time founders and digital agencies to funded startups, manufacturers and established SMEs. If you run a business and need commercial legal clarity, we should talk.

Do you offer flat-fee pricing?

Yes. Most engagements are quoted as a fixed fee agreed before any work begins, so you know the cost up front. Ongoing advisory is available on a monthly retainer.

Can you review or draft contracts?

Absolutely — from NDAs and MSAs to shareholder agreements, employment contracts and complex commercial terms. You get plain-English explanations, not just redlines.

Do you offer consultations before hiring?

Every engagement starts with a free, no-obligation consultation to scope the work and confirm we are the right fit before you commit.

Are you available for ongoing legal support?

Yes. Many clients keep me on a monthly retainer as their on-demand legal partner, so support scales up and down as the business grows.

Do you work with clients remotely?

Yes. I work with clients across Canada by video and email, with the same responsiveness and clarity as an in-person relationship.
