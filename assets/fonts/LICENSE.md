# Bundled fonts

These woff2 files (latin subset) are redistributed under the
**SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1** — free for commercial use, bundling and
web embedding.

- **Cormorant Garamond** — designed by Christian Thalmann (Catharsis Fonts).
- **Lato** — designed by Łukasz Dziedzic (tyPoland).

Full license text: https://openfontlicense.org
Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com).

The fonts are self-hosted (rather than loaded from a CDN) for privacy and
performance. To add weights or language subsets, download them from Google
Fonts and add matching `@font-face` rules in `styles.css`.
