Our story

Built on callbacks we never got

Plumbo Plumbing & Heating started in 2009 with one truck, one journeyman ticket, and a simple bet: if you quote honestly, show up when you said you would, and stand behind the work, Regina will keep you busy forever. Seventeen years later, most of our jobs still come from referrals and repeat customers.

We're a local, family-owned crew — not a franchise call centre. The person who answers the phone knows the person who shows up at your door, and both of them answer to the name on the truck.

  • Licensed & insuredSaskatchewan journeyman plumbers, gas-fitting licensed, $5M liability coverage.
  • Upfront flat-rate pricingThe price we quote is the price you pay — approved by you before work starts.
  • Written workmanship warrantyEvery repair backed in writing. If it fails, we return and fix it free.
