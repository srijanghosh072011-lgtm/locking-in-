Plumbing Repair in Regina, SK

Leaks, drips, running toilets, mystery noises in the walls — diagnosed and fixed the same day, at a price you approve first.

Quick answer

Plumbo provides same-day plumbing repair across Regina and area, typically arriving within 2–4 hours of your call (under 60 minutes for emergencies). Every repair is quoted flat-rate before work begins and backed by a written workmanship warranty from licensed Saskatchewan journeyman plumbers.

A small drip wastes water; an ignored one rots cabinets, feeds mould, and turns a $150 fix into a $5,000 insurance claim. Our repair trucks carry the parts that solve about 90% of household plumbing failures on the first visit, so most Regina repairs are finished the same day you call.

We repair every fixture and line in the house: kitchen and bathroom faucets, toilets, supply lines, shut-off valves, garburators, dishwashers hookups, outdoor taps, and the pipes that connect them all.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can you get to my Regina home for a repair?
Same-day for most repair calls placed before 2 PM, and typically within 60 minutes for genuine emergencies like burst pipes. We serve all Regina neighbourhoods plus White City, Pilot Butte, Balgonie, Lumsden and area.
How much does a plumbing repair cost in Regina?
Most common repairs (running toilet, leaking faucet, seized shut-off) land between $150 and $450. We quote a flat price on-site before starting — the number you approve is the number on the invoice.
Are your plumbers licensed and insured?
Yes. Every technician is a licensed Saskatchewan journeyman plumber or a registered apprentice working under one, and the company carries $5M in liability insurance plus WCB coverage.
Do you guarantee your repair work?
Every repair comes with a written workmanship warranty. If the same failure recurs within the warranty period, we return and fix it at no charge.
