Water Heater Repair & Installation in Regina, SK

No hot water in a Saskatchewan winter isn't an inconvenience, it's an emergency. We repair or replace tank and tankless heaters, usually the same day.

Quick answer

We repair and install gas, electric and tankless water heaters all over Regina. Most repairs get your hot water back the same day, and a full tank swap is usually done inside 24 hours — sized right for your household and our cold Saskatchewan water, with the permits and gas-fitting handled by a licensed tech. You don't lift a finger.

Our tap water shows up close to 5°C for a good chunk of the year, which is about as hard as a water heater has to work anywhere in North America. That's why tanks don't last as long here, and why sizing matters so much — go too small and you'll be taking lukewarm showers every January.

We work on every common brand, fix what's worth fixing, and tell you straight when a new one is the cheaper call over five years. Tankless upgrade, power-vent, or a plain old tank — we install it to code and pull the permit for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long should a water heater last around here?
Figure 8–12 years for a standard tank in Regina's hard water. Flush it every year and swap the anode rod around year 5 and you'll push toward the long end. Tankless units go 15–20 years with regular descaling.
Should I repair it or just replace it?
Quick rule of thumb: if the repair's going to cost more than a third of a new one and the tank's past 8 years old, replace it. A leak from the tank itself is the end of the road — but controls, elements and valves are usually worth fixing.
What does a new water heater cost installed?
Ballpark: a standard 50-gallon gas tank runs $2,200–$3,200, power-vent $2,800–$4,000, and tankless $4,500–$7,000 once you factor in venting and any gas line work. We nail down the flat price before we touch anything.
Are tankless heaters actually worth it in Saskatchewan?
For most homes, yeah — endless hot water and lower running costs — as long as it's cold-climate rated and sized for our near-freezing water. There's a full rundown in our Tips section if you want the details.
